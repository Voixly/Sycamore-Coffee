Vendor Requirements

  • Obtain all applicable permits and licenses from the county health department.
  • Submit this vendor application, vendor agreement (that comes after this application) and receive an approval email.
  • Admission will be decided on an individual basis according to the needs of the market.

FAQs

When are the markets? Magnolia Local Market: 1st Saturdays 9am–1pm

Do you limit competing products? Yes, we do our best to not schedule duplicating products and ensure the success of our returning vendors.

What is the fee to reserve space in a market? You will have the option to reserve a space after you have completed this application and the vendor agreement. All of our markets offer a tiered payment option. Each fee comes with a $2 online processing fee to reserve a 10' × 10' booth space.
